Return the ball with your paddle: its middle sends the ball back straight, its ends at a steep angle. The longer a rally lasts, the faster the ball.	用球拍把球打回去：拍子中间把球直直打回，两端打出斜角。来回越久，球越快。
Knock the puck into the other goal. Your mallet stays in your half; hit the puck on the move to send it flying.	把冰球打进对方球门。你的球槌只能在自己半场；边移动边击球，球就会飞出去。
Drive through the maze and hit the other tank. Shells bounce off the walls: watch out for your own!	在迷宫里开坦克，击中对方。炮弹会在墙上反弹：小心别打到自己！
Steer your light cycle and make the other one crash into a wall or a trail. It never stops and never turns back.	驾驶你的光速摩托，让对方撞上墙壁或光轨。摩托从不停下，也不能掉头。
Wait for DRAW!, then press your key: the first one wins. Too soon loses the round.	等到出现「拔枪！」再按你的键，先按的获胜。按太快就输了这一局。
Tap your key fast to pull the rope. Pull the flag past your line.	快速连按你的键，把旗子拉过你的线。
Jump with up or your key. Hit the ball over the net and don't let it land on your side.	用上或你的键跳起。把球打过网，别让它落在你这边。
Push the other one out of the ring. Your key dashes. The ring shrinks.	把对方推出场地。你的键是冲撞。场地会变小。
Tap up or your key to fly through the gaps. Don't touch the walls or the edges.	按上或你的键飞过缺口。别碰到墙或上下边缘。
Up and down aim. Hold your key to draw the bow, let go to shoot. Three hits win.	上下调整角度。按住你的键拉弓，松开射箭。命中三次获胜。
Your key throws at the other one. Dodge their balls. Three hits win.	你的键向对方投球。躲开对方的球。命中三次获胜。
Push the ball into the other goal. Near it, your key kicks it at the goal.	把球推进对方球门。靠近时，你的键把球踢向球门。
Dodge the falling rocks. Both lanes get the same ones. Last one standing wins.	躲开落下的石头。两边落下的石头相同。最后留下的获胜。
Hold your key to lower the hook, let go to reel in. Land a fish first.	按住你的键放下鱼钩，松开收线。先钓到鱼的获胜。
Watch the arrows, then repeat them. First to finish without a mistake wins.	看好箭头，再照着按一遍。先全部按对的获胜，按错就输。
