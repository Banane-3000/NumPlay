Wait for DRAW, then press your action key. A false start loses the round. Simultaneous shots draw.	等待开枪信号，再按行动键。抢先开枪会输；同时开枪则平局。
Tap your action key to pull the rope. Release between pulls: holding does not repeat. Drag the flag past your line.	反复按下并松开行动键来拉绳。长按不会重复。把旗子拉过你的线。
Move around the pitch and kick with your action key. Push the ball into the other goal; rebounds stay in play.	在球场移动，用行动键踢球。把球踢进对方球门；球会在边线反弹。
Move left and right; Up or your action key jumps. Keep the ball off your floor and send it over the net.	左右移动，用上键或行动键跳跃。把球打过球网，别让它落在己方地上。
Move in your half and throw with your action key. Dodge incoming balls. Three hits knock a player out.	在己方半场移动，用行动键投球。躲避来球，被击中三次就出局。
Move left and right to dodge the falling rocks. Both lanes get the same obstacles. Stay alive longer than your rival.	左右移动躲避陨石。两条赛道的障碍完全相同，比对手坚持更久。
Tap Up or your action key to boost your rocket. Fly through the gaps; avoid the walls. Both pilots face the same course.	按上键或行动键推进火箭。穿过空隙，避开墙壁。两位飞行员面对同一赛道。
Move inside the ring. Your action key dashes and pushes your rival. Stay inside: the ring gradually shrinks.	在圆环里移动。行动键冲刺并推开对手。别出界，圆环会逐渐缩小。
Move your rod left and right. Hold your action key to lower the hook; release to reel in a fish. Catch five first.	左右移动鱼竿。按住行动键放下鱼钩，松开收竿。先钓到五条鱼获胜。
